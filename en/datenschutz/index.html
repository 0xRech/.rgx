Privacy at a glance

The RGX project website uses no analytics tools, no marketing cookies and no external fonts. The site is static and contains no contact form.

Hosting & server logs

The website is hosted by netcup. Technically required server log data such as IP address, timestamp, requested resource, browser information and referrer may be processed when the site is accessed.

Local preferences

If you switch languages, that preference may be stored locally in the browser using localStorage. RGX no longer offers a theme selector; the website is delivered exclusively in the RGX dark design.

The full Rech Group privacy notice contains the general information about the controller, hosting, legal bases and data subject rights.
